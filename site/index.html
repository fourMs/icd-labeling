Connect to the drive

The site reaches the internal drive through a small helper that runs on your computer. It logs in with your own SSH account, and nothing leaves your machine except to the drive.

  1. Download sftp_bridge.py and install its one dependency: 
    pip install paramiko
  2. Start it and log in with your UiO account (Authenticator approval and password are typed in the terminal, not here): 
    python3 sftp_bridge.py --user your-uio-username
    It opens the Intercontinental Database folder on login.uio.no. Pass another user@host:/path to use a different folder.
